# Landing page

Mode: Persuade. User delegated direction selection and code-first execution.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the relationships between money decisions tangible, using a transit-map grammar rather than ornamental finance chrome.
OWN-WORLD: Manrope, indigo #4948c9, ink #20214a, lilac #e8e9fc, white canvas, readable destinations and crisp routing lines.
STORY: Understand income, spending and future goals; explore a small savings change; open the existing ecosystem or begin onboarding.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned large headline and action; right-side money route with income, account and three destinations. Primary action stays beside the explanation.
FORM: City transit map, grounded candidate 5, seed 823af044. Seven considered families: account ledger, stream diagram, departures board, inventory sheet, transit map, floor plan, mixing desk. Foreign collage, film and toy-brand systems lose financial clarity; their discipline raises color commitment and route legibility.
INTERACTION: The savings toggle updates conserved monthly allocations and an annual difference. No autonomous motion. Existing graph behavior remains unchanged.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
